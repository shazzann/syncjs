The most expensive bug is a feature nobody asked for. You find it at launch, and by then it has been designed, built and tested.

Weekly demos catch it in week two instead.

## How a demo runs

1. **15 minutes, every Friday.** Same time, same link.
2. **Working software only.** No slides, no mockups. If it isn't running, it isn't shown.
3. **You drive.** We hand over the screen and watch where you hesitate.
4. **Decisions are written down** and land in the next week's plan.

## What it changes

Feedback arrives while a change still costs an hour, not a week. And you always know where the project stands, without asking for a status report.

The demo also keeps us honest: there's nowhere to hide a week without progress.
