Most software quotes are guesses. The agency hears a 30-minute description, multiplies by a risk factor and hopes. When the guess is wrong, someone pays: the client in change requests, or the team in unpaid weekends.

We'd rather not guess. So every project starts with a two-week paid discovery.

## What happens in two weeks

- **Day 1-3:** we talk to the people who will use the software, not only the people buying it.
- **Day 4-7:** we map the workflows, the data and the systems it has to talk to.
- **Day 8-10:** we click-test the riskiest screens with real users and write the scope.

## What you get

A written scope, a timeline with weekly milestones and a fixed price. It's yours to keep. If you take it to another team, that's fine.

> A fixed price only works when both sides agree on what "done" means.

## Why it's paid

Because it's real work, and because it keeps both sides honest. You get our full attention, and we only quote what we understand.
