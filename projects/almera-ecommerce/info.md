An online store for almera, a Sri Lankan boutique selling Pakistani lawn suits, shalwar kameez and lehengas. Customers shop on their phones, and the owner runs orders, stock and products from a mobile-friendly admin console.

## Problem
almera sold through Instagram, so every order meant messages back and forth to check stock, sizes, prices and payment. There was no catalogue to browse, no live stock and no single place to track orders.

## What we built
- A mobile-first store with categories, filters and product pages offering stitched or unstitched options and sizes
- Checkout with cash on delivery, bank transfer and card payments, built for Sri Lankan districts and phone numbers
- Live stock tracking by size and option, with items held during card payment so nothing is oversold
- An admin console for orders, deposits, bank slips, refunds, products, categories and promo codes
- Order tracking and confirmation emails for customers
- Fast photo delivery through a CDN, with smaller copies for phones
